Settings

Video
Transport Priority i A "transport" is the connection method between your PC and GoPro. The app tries each one in order from top to bottom, automatically switching to the next if one fails. Green highlight = currently active.

Use the arrows to reorder. The first available transport will be used automatically.

USB i Fastest and most reliable. Plug your GoPro into your PC with a USB cable. No network required. Recommended as your primary connection. COHN i Camera On Home Network — streams over your home Wi-Fi without a USB cable. Requires one-time setup (see the Setup page). Great wireless fallback when USB is unavailable. WiFi AP i Connects to the GoPro's own built-in Wi-Fi hotspot. No USB needed, but your PC temporarily joins the camera's network instead of your home network. Slowest option.
Manual Switch i Force an immediate switch to a specific connection method right now, bypassing the automatic priority order. Useful for testing or if auto-detection picks the wrong transport.

Force-connect via a specific transport. Highlighted button = currently active.

General
Auto-Start on Login i Automatically launch GoMaxWebcam when you log into your computer. Your GoPro will be ready as a webcam as soon as you sit down — no manual start needed.
Launch GoMaxWebcam when you log in
BLE Wake: Always-On i Keeps Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) active so the app can detect and wake your camera instantly. Turn this off if you want to save your camera's battery — the app will still connect, just slightly slower.
Keep Bluetooth active for instant camera wake
Debug Logging i Records detailed technical information to log files. Only enable this when troubleshooting a problem — it creates much larger log files and may slightly affect performance. Leave it off for normal use.
Verbose logging for troubleshooting (log files grow faster)
